## Idea
Every day, people lose important items like wallets, mobile phones, ID cards, keys, bags, and documents in public places such as colleges, railway stations, malls, and buses. Even if someone finds these items, there is no common platform to connect the person who lost the item with the person who found it. Existing lost-and-found systems mostly depend on manual searching and exact descriptions, making the process slow and less effective.Our solution is an *AI-powered Lost and Found Platform* that helps match lost and found items quickly. Users can upload a photo or give a simple description of the item. The AI compares images and understands descriptions to find the best possible matches, even if the words used are different. It can also identify text on items, such as ID cards or labels, using OCR technology. The platform sends instant notifications when a matching item is found, making the recovery process faster, easier, and more reliable for everyone.
